Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about models, security, latency, and microphones.

Can I run J.A.R.V.I.S Plus 100% offline with zero cloud APIs? ›
Yes! You can run completely air-gapped on your local hardware. Set OLLAMA_BASE_URL=http://localhost:11434/v1 for local LLM inference (e.g. Llama 3.3 or Qwen 2.5), use local Faster-Whisper on CPU/GPU for STT, and use local Kokoro/Piper or Edge-TTS. No telemetry or audio ever leaves your computer.
Why does the browser ask to accept a self-signed certificate? ›
Modern mobile browsers (iOS Safari, Android Chrome) strictly require an HTTPS / TLS encrypted connection to grant microphone permissions over a local area network (LAN). Running ./setup.sh automatically generates secure TLS certificates for your machine so your phone or laptop can access the mic without complex domain routing.
How does Full-Turn Speech Barge-In work? ›
Unlike basic assistants where you have to wait for the bot to finish talking, J.A.R.V.I.S Plus monitors human voice energy in real time via Silero VAD. If you speak over Jarvis (e.g. saying "Stop", "Never mind", or wake-word "Jarvis"), the server instantly terminates audio playback, issues a stop signal to running background tool calls, and starts listening to your new intent immediately.
How do I get studio-grade human emotional voices? ›
While the default Microsoft Edge-TTS voices are completely free and sound natural (including authentic native Persian voices FaridNeural and DilaraNeural), you can plug in your ELEVENLABS_API_KEY or SONIOX_API_KEY in .env to stream hyper-realistic, emotionally expressive cinema-grade audio.
Can I access J.A.R.V.I.S Plus from my iPhone or Android device? ›
Yes! The HUD is responsive and designed with 100dvh viewport metrics specifically for mobile screens. Simply navigate to https://<YOUR-HOST-IP>:8766/hud/?token=<YOUR-TOKEN> in Safari or Chrome on your phone.
